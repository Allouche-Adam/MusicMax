MusicMax is ready

Spotify Web now has lyrics for the songs it was missing. Here's how it works.

Open Spotify Web
1

Play any song

On open.spotify.com. When Spotify says it has no lyrics, MusicMax looks them up for you.

2

Click the mic

The lyrics button in the player bar works again. Or press L. Click any line to jump the song there.

3

Make it yours

The gear in the lyrics view, or the MusicMax icon in Chrome's toolbar, opens every setting.
