# MusicMax privacy policy

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

MusicMax is a browser extension that shows lyrics in the Spotify web player
(open.spotify.com). This policy explains what data it handles.

## Summary

MusicMax has no account, no server of its own, no analytics and no ads. The developer
does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data.

## What the extension reads

On open.spotify.com only, it reads the playing song's **title, artist, length,
position and cover image address** from the page, so it can find and display lyrics.

## What is sent, and to whom

- **LRCLIB (lrclib.net)**: the song's title, artist and length, to find its lyrics.
  This happens for songs Spotify has no lyrics for, and when you search manually.
- **Genius (genius.com) and NetEase (music.163.com)**: only if you turn on "Also
  search Genius & NetEase" in the settings (off by default). Your manual search text,
  and for Japanese songs the title and artist, are sent to find lyrics.
- **Spotify's image server (i.scdn.co)**: the cover image already shown on the page
  is downloaded to pick the lyrics' background colors and to draw share images.

No information about you (name, email, Spotify account, listening history) is
included. Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy.

## What is stored on your device

Your settings and up to 500 found lyrics are saved in your browser's extension storage
so songs load instantly next time, plus a log of the last 300 lookups for
troubleshooting. This never leaves your device unless you press "Copy debug log"
yourself. You can clear saved lyrics and the log from the extension's popup;
uninstalling the extension deletes everything.

## Translation

Translations use Chrome's built-in, on-device translator. Lyrics are translated on
your computer and are not sent to any translation service.

## Sharing a line

"Share a line" images are made on your computer and only copied to your clipboard or
saved where you choose.
